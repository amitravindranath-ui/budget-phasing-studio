Composite Score Engine

Built directly from your "Month x Week(forced) Pivot" and "Month x Weekday Pivot" sheets — adjust weights and see scores recompute live

Formula: Composite Score = wEff×Efficiency_norm + wCAC×(1−CAC_norm) + wRen×Renewal_norm + wAct×Activation_norm. Renewal = mean(W1, W2, W3 Renewals). Each metric is min–max normalized across the 5 week-buckets (or 7 weekdays) within its quarter, CAC is inverted since lower cost is better. Default weights (40/20/30/10) reproduce your documented Table 1/Table 2 scores almost exactly — see the Validation panel below.

Suggested scores sum to exactly 100 for the 5 Week (forced) buckets, and separately sum to exactly 100 for the 7 Weekdays, while keeping adjacent-step differences capped at ≤10.

Flat / Historical vs Smoothed Model

How efficiently the smoothed model deploys the same real budget vs a Flat or Historical Actual spend baseline

Budget in Scope is the one real ₹ number here. Each day carries an Efficiency Index (its original composite-score-derived weight, normalized to mean = 1 per month). Efficiency Multiplier = Σ(spend × Efficiency Index) ÷ Budget in Scope.

Flat Spend baseline is always exactly 1.00x by construction (equal spend every day). Historical Actual Spend baseline reconstructs how spend was actually distributed historically — using the real average spend per week-bucket and weekday from your pivot sheets — rescaled to fit this budget. Unlike Flat, its multiplier is not fixed at 1.00x, so it directly shows whether your past actual spend pattern already leaned toward efficient days, or whether the new Smoothed model genuinely improves on how budget was really being spent.
